# Northwind Labs Leave Policy

This is a fictional sample policy for testing cited-docs-agent.

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave builds up at 2 days per month, starting from the joining date. Part-time employees get annual leave in proportion to their contracted hours.

Annual leave must be requested in the HR portal at least 7 days in advance for breaks longer than 3 days. Your manager approves or declines the request within 2 working days.

## Carry-over

You can carry over up to 5 unused days of annual leave into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, or they expire. Unused days beyond those 5 are not paid out, except when you leave the company.

## Sick leave

Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Tell your manager before 10 am on the first day you are unwell. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver gets 26 weeks of paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver gets 4 weeks of paid parental leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption. Parental leave is available after 6 months of employment.

## Public holidays

Each office publishes its list of 12 public holidays in December for the following year. Remote employees follow the list for the office closest to where they live.
