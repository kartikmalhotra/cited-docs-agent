# Northwind Labs Expense Policy

This is a fictional sample policy for testing cited-docs-agent.

## Travel

Book all flights and hotels through the company travel portal. Flights shorter than 6 hours are economy class. For flights of 6 hours or more, premium economy is allowed with manager approval. Hotels are capped at ₹7,000 per night in India and $180 per night abroad.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance is ₹2,500 for travel within India and $75 for travel abroad. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Team dinners need written approval from a director before the event.

## Claims and receipts

Submit every claim with its receipts in the expense tool within 30 days of spending the money. Claims older than 90 days are rejected. Any single expense above ₹10,000 needs your manager's approval before you spend it. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Home office

New employees get a one-time home office allowance of ₹15,000 for a chair, desk or monitor. The allowance must be claimed within the first 3 months of joining. Internet bills are reimbursed up to ₹1,500 per month for employees who work remotely full-time.
